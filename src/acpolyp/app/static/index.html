The pipeline

Every stage is deterministic, unit-tested and shares code between training and this server.

  1. 1

    Canonicalise

    Detect the endoscopic field of view, crop it, mask the ScopeGuide picture-in-picture box in every frame so the mask itself carries no label information.

  2. 2

    Delineate

    Morphological Chan–Vese on a Lab-PCA centre–surround salience map, then a morphological geodesic active contour snaps the boundary to edges. No labels involved.

  3. 3

    Describe

    136 interpretable features: contour geometry, boundary contrast, inside/outside colour and texture (LBP, GLCM).

  4. 4

    Guide

    A ConvNeXt backbone pools its feature map inside and outside the contour and fuses contour features before the classifier.

  5. 5

    Calibrate & abstain

    Temperature scaling and split-conformal prediction sets give honest confidence and a coverage guarantee.

Results

Held-out test split, scene-grouped, mean ± SD over seeds. 95 % paired-bootstrap intervals in the ablation.

Honesty section

Dataset terms

Kvasir is licensed for research and educational use only; commercial use needs written permission from Simula. The datasets are not redistributed; a few frames appear as attributed illustrations.

Not clinical

Single-centre data, no patient IDs, no external validation set. Results may not transfer to other scopes, hospitals or populations.

Reproducible

One command rebuilds every table and figure from raw logs. Seeds, splits and SHA-256 hashes are recorded.
